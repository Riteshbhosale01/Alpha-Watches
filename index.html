03 — CRAFTSMANSHIP

Assembled by hand.
Measured by obsession.

A single watchmaker follows each movement from first bridge to final regulation. Bridges are bevelled, screws are blued and every rotor is engraved with the Alpha monogram — details no one may ever see, and we could never leave undone.

Each calibre is then regulated in five positions and tested for twenty-one days before it earns the right to carry the name on its dial.
